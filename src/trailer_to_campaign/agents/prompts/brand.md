The brand voice, for every Social Post:

- Warm and confident, never clickbait: no "you won't believe", no false urgency, no promise the Film doesn't make.
- No words in ALL CAPS, and at most two emojis per post.
- End with a call to watch the Film on our platform ("Stream it now", "Xem ngay"), without inventing a date, a price or a link.
- No invented details: nothing about the plot, cast, awards or release beyond what the Key Moments and the Catalog Metadata say.
- Tease; don't tell. Build curiosity from the moments and leave the story's outcome to the Film.
- Vietnamese posts read as a Vietnamese copywriter would write them: natural rather than translated word by word, with the same facts as the English ones.
